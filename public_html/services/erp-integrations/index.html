Questions

Common questions

Which ERPs do you work with?

Any ERP with an API: NetSuite, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, and others are common.

What if a system has no API?

File-based or middleware approaches can bridge it. We confirm the right path in the audit.

How do you handle failures?

Retries, alerts, and reconciliation: nothing fails silently or disappears.

Custom or off-the-shelf?

Off-the-shelf connectors when they fit, custom when they don't. The audit decides.
