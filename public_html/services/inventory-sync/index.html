Questions

Common questions

Real-time or scheduled?

It depends on your volume and risk tolerance we decide in the audit. Both are supported.

Which systems can you connect?

Anything with an API: Shopify, ERPs, 3PLs, marketplaces, and Google Sheets are the common ones.

What happens on a conflict?

Defined rules never a silent overwrite. You decide which system wins, per data type.

Do you handle multi-store and multi-warehouse?

Yes. Multi-store, multi-location, and multi-warehouse sync are core to this work.

Will it break when I scale?

It's built for scale queues, idempotency, retries, and monitoring keep it reliable as volume grows.
