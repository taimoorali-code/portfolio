About

I'm Taimoor Ali Shopify integration & ecommerce automation specialist.

I help growing Shopify, Plus, DTC and subscription brands replace operational chaos with systems that run themselves.

What I actually do

Most growing brands hit the same wall: the team becomes the glue holding operations together. Inventory drifts across channels. Orders get re-keyed between systems. Subscriptions run on workarounds. I build the integrations and automations that remove the human from that loop so the back office runs on its own and the team scales output instead of headcount.

Why work with me instead of a developer or a consultant

A Shopify developer builds what you spec, but won't tell you the architecture is the real problem. An operations consultant hands you a strategy, then hands the build to someone else and projects die in that gap. I'm both: I diagnose the operational chaos and engineer the system that ends it. One person, one line of accountability, no handoff.

How I work

Engagements usually start with an Operations Audit — a fixed-fee diagnosis that maps your systems and hands you a prioritized automation roadmap. From there, fixed-scope builds implement the roadmap, and an ongoing partnership keeps everything healthy as you grow. I price on the value of the problem solved, not the hours.

The work behind it

Selected work includes WhisperSheet (an owned inventory-sync SaaS that cut manual inventory work 80%), INK (a verified-delivery system that cut fulfillment disputes 90%), Neesh (a multi-vendor B2B marketplace), and Shopify Plus development and integrations for brands including Elie Saab, within my assigned scope. The throughline is the same: disconnected systems, made to run as one.
