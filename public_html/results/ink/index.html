INK · Case study

Eliminated fake deliveries and cut fulfillment disputes 90%.

Client
SaaS · Shopify app
Result
~90% fewer fulfillment disputes
Systems
Shopify app, NFC verification, warehouse app
Role
Development & implementation lead

Context

INK is a verified-delivery system for Shopify brands confirming real package handoffs and keeping inventory airtight from pack to doorstep.

The problem

Brands faced disputed and fraudulent 'non-deliveries' that cost money and eroded trust, with no reliable proof of handoff.

The operational bottleneck

There was no verifiable link between a shipped package and a confirmed real-world delivery, and inventory accuracy degraded between warehouse and doorstep.

Solution architecture

NFC-tagged packages confirm genuine handoffs, while a warehouse app keeps inventory accurate from pack to delivery closing the gap between digital records and physical reality.

Results

Outcome

~90% reduction in fulfillment disputes, near-elimination of fake-delivery claims, and tighter inventory accuracy.

Business impact

Recovered revenue lost to disputes, protected brand trust, and gave operations verifiable delivery data.

Integrations involved

▸Shopify app framework & APIs
▸NFC tag verification
▸Warehouse / inventory app
▸Webhooks
