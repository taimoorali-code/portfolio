Neesh · Case study

Turning a print magazine into a B2B Shopify marketplace.

Client
B2B marketplace
Result
Launched multi-vendor marketplace
Systems
Shopify API, multi-vendor logic, Node.js
Role
Lead developer, end to end

Context

Neesh transformed from a print publication into a digital B2B marketplace connecting publishers, retailers, and inventory under one storefront.

The problem

A traditional publisher needed to become a multi-sided marketplace, connecting many vendors, retailers, and inventory sources in one Shopify-based platform.

The operational bottleneck

There was no system to manage multi-vendor catalogs, inventory, and order routing across publishers and retailers at once.

Solution architecture

A custom Shopify integration with multi-vendor logic links publishers, retailers, and inventory under a single storefront, with the order and inventory routing to support it.

Results

Outcome

A functioning B2B marketplace serving multiple vendors and retailers.

Business impact

Opened a new digital revenue model for a legacy publisher and created a scalable multi-vendor platform.

Integrations involved

▸Shopify API
▸Custom multi-vendor logic
▸Node.js backend
▸Inventory / order routing
