The problem

If this is your operation, we should talk.

  • Your inventory is never quite right across channels.
  • Orders get copied by hand between Shopify and your ERP or 3PL.
  • Your subscription setup is held together with workarounds nobody wants to touch.
  • Someone on your team is in a spreadsheet at midnight, again.
  • No two systems ever seem to agree on the same number.

Every one of these is a tax on your margin and a ceiling on your growth. You don't have a marketing problem. You have an operations problem and unlike most of what slows a brand down, this one is completely solvable.

The shift

I build the systems that make the manual work disappear.

Most brands try to fix operational chaos by hiring more people or bolting on another app. Both make it worse more humans and more tools mean more glue to maintain. The durable fix is architecture: systems engineered to talk to each other so data flows on its own.

I do both halves of that. I diagnose what's actually broken in your operation, then I engineer the integrations and automations that fix it. No strategy deck handed off to someone else to build. No developer building exactly what you spec'd without telling you the spec was the problem. One person who owns the outcome from diagnosis to deployment.
