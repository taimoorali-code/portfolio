WhisperSheet · Case study

Cut manual inventory work 80% for multi-store brands.

Client
SaaS · multi-store Shopify brands
Result
80% less manual inventory work
Systems
Shopify, Google Sheets, background jobs
Role
Lead architect & developer (owned product)

Context

WhisperSheet is a SaaS product built and owned by Taimoor serving Shopify brands that run part of their operations out of Google Sheets and need those sheets and their stores to stay in perfect agreement.

The problem

Brands managing inventory across multiple stores and spreadsheets faced constant drift counts in Sheets and Shopify never matched, forcing late-night manual edits and risking oversells.

The operational bottleneck

Inventory truth was split between Shopify and Google Sheets, with humans acting as the sync mechanism slow, error-prone, and impossible to scale as store count grew.

Solution architecture

A synchronization engine keeps Shopify and Google Sheets in lockstep: a defined source of truth, automated two-way updates, conflict handling, and error alerting removing the human from the loop entirely.

Results

Outcome

~80% reduction in manual inventory work, the end of midnight spreadsheet reconciliation, and reliable, scalable sync across multiple stores.

Business impact

Teams reclaimed 20+ hours a week, reduced oversell risk, and gained an inventory system that scales with store count instead of headcount.

Integrations involved

▸Shopify Admin / GraphQL API
▸Webhooks
▸Google Sheets API
▸Background job queue
▸Monitoring & alerting
